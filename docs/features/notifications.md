# Notification System

Notify Rush keeps you instantly informed about important events happening in your Shopify store. You’ll receive valuable insights—such as unshipped order details, refunds, and more—helping you make confident, informed decisions. All notifications are delivered based on the preferences you configure within the app.




## Key Benefits

- Stay informed about critical order events
- Proactive alerts for unshipped orders
- Track refunds in real-time

## Notification Types

### Unshipped Orders
Unshipped Order Notifications alert you about orders that remain unshipped for a specified number of days, based on the settings you configure in the app.
![Alt text description](/img/unshippedOrders.png)

Click on View Details button to check the detailed information about unshipped orders.

![Alt text description](/img/unshipped-detailed.png)

### Refund Orders
Refund Order Notifications alert you when orders have been refunded within a specific timeframe, based on the settings you configure in the app.
![Alt text description](/img/orderRefunds.png)

Click on View Details button to check the detailed information about refunded orders.
![Alt text description](/img/refund-details.png)

### Product-Specific Refunds
Specific Product Refund Order Notifications inform you how many times a particular product has been refunded within a selected timeframe, based on the settings you configure in the app.
![Alt text description](/img/specificproductrefund.png)

Click on View Details button to check the detailed information about refunds of a specific product.
![Alt text description](/img/specificproductrefund-detail.png)
